## Environment limitation (important)
This verifier environment cannot display images: every `view` call on a PNG/JPEG comes back as
"Tool output failed to proccess text" with no pixels (reproduced with a 120×67 PNG, a 300×168 JPEG and 620px JPEGs).
So the *human* half of A3 ("open every anchor screenshot and state what is visible") and D1 ("open and review each")
could not be performed. Every screenshot was still captured and analysed programmatically instead:
per-anchor 15-ray scene casts (materials/meshes in view, centre distance, embedded-in-solid, floor drop) plus
per-image luminance statistics (black/blank-frame detection). A3 and D1 are reported PARTIAL, not PASS.
